## Periodicity (critical)

All monetary variables are stored as **MONTHLY** amounts in EUR. Each is derived from an
annual SILC variable as `py01Xg / 12 * hx010` (the `hx010` factor adjusts to the reference
income period). Hours (`lhw`) are weekly. Months (`liwmy`, `lunmy`, `lpemy`) are months/year.

Dimensional sanity check for our chosen-row reconstruction:
`yem_chosen = lhw × yivwg × (52/12) = (hours/week) × (EUR/hour) × (weeks/month) = EUR/month` ✓

## `les` — Economic Status (THE authoritative coding)

| code | meaning |
|---:|---|
| 0 | Pre-school |
| 1 | Farmer |
| 2 | Employer or self-employed |
| 3 | Employee |
| 4 | Pensioner |
| 5 | Unemployed |
| 6 | Student |
| 7 | Inactive |
| 8 | Sick or Disabled |
| 9 | Other |
| 10 | Family worker |

**Employed-like** = {1, 2, 3, 10} (earning categories).
**Not employed** = {0, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9}.

FR derivation: `les=0` from `dec==1` (pre-primary ed.); rest from `pl031` (self-defined
activity) and `pl040` (employee vs self-employed split).

## `yem` family — the three layers of employee income

- **`yem`**  = gross MONTHLY employee cash/near-cash income = `py010g/12 × hx010`. **Includes overtime + bonuses.**
- **`yem00`** = base employment income, **imputed** from yem, hours, and months. Captures *only the base pay* at normal hours.
- **`yemxp`** = overtime/bonus residual = `yem − yem00`.
- **`yempv`** = previous-year employment income, **imputed by inverting the unemployment-benefit formula** (used for newly unemployed deciders).
- **`yivwg`** = imputed HOURLY wage (€/hour). Generated by do-file `13b_Wage` for everyone aged 18–65, including non-workers. This is the "shadow wage" used to construct counterfactual earnings in the B-pool.

The B-pool chosen-row reconstruction `yem_ch = lhw × yivwg × 52/12` corresponds to
**`yem00`-equivalent**, not `yem`. This is why we observe:
- `lhw_chosen == lhw_obs` exactly (hours preserved)
- `yivwg_chosen == yivwg_obs` exactly (wage preserved)
- `yem_chosen ≈ lhw × yivwg × 52/12` to float precision
- `yem_chosen` can differ from survey `yem_obs` by thousands of EUR for workers with high `yemxp` (overtime/bonuses)

This is BY DESIGN, not a bug. The estimator sees an internally consistent choice set
where all alternatives — chosen and simulated — use the same `lhw × yivwg` formula.
See `feedback-bpool-chosen-row-is-reconstructed`.

## `les` vs `yem` disagreement

The DRD records `les` (self-declared activity) and `yem` (recorded annual earnings ÷ 12)
as SEPARATE survey items that occasionally disagree:

- **Employees with zero recorded earnings** (`les=3 & yem=0 & lhw>0`): survey gap (e.g.
  started job late). B-pool correctly recovers them as workers via `lhw × yivwg`.
- **Unemployed/inactive with residual earnings** (`les ∈ {5,7} & yem>0 & lhw=0`):
  trailing income (final paycheck after job ended). B-pool correctly marks them as
  non-workers via `lhw=0`.

See `feedback-bpool-les-vs-yem-flips-are-structural` for the 280 cases observed.

## French-specific benefit decomposition

`bfa` (family) is split into: `bch00` (Allocation Familiale), `bchcc` (CLCA parental),
`bched` (ARS back-to-school), `bchlg` (CF large family), `bchot` (residual).

`bun` (unemployment) is split into: `bunct` (ARE contributory) and `bunmt` (ASS means-tested).

`bsa` (social assistance) is split into: `bsa00` (RSA basic), `bsaoa` (ASPA old-age means-tested),
`bsaot` (residual exclusion benefits).

`pdi` (disability) is split into: `bdi` (AAH adult handicap allowance) and `pdi00` (invalidity pension residual).

`poa` (old-age) rebalanced so `poa = poa00 + bsaoa`.

`bho` (housing) split into `bhotn` (tenant housing benefit) and `bhoot` (other housing benefit).

## Cross-cuts on disposable income

- **`yds`** = SILC-reported disposable income = `(hy020 × hy025)/12 × hx010`. The survey's
  own aggregate, stored as a monthly value per HH (only on the household-head row).
- **`ils_dispy`** = EUROMOD-simulated disposable income — recomputed from gross inputs by
  the policy system being applied. **Differs from `yds`** because EUROMOD computes
  benefits and taxes from the rules, not from the survey.
- The canonical `fr_{year}.parquet` stores `ils_dispy` computed under the SAME-year system
  (e.g. 2016 data through FR_2016 policy). The B-pool prices under the **t-1 system** (FR_2015
  for 2016 data) by design (the "opportunity year" concept).

## When to consult the DRD

Before answering any question about:
- whether a column should exist on input vs. output
- what units a monetary variable is in
- how a benefit/tax variable is derived
- whether `les` matches `yem` or `lhw` and why
- the difference between `yem`, `yem00`, `yemxp`, `yempv`, `yivwg`

Always open `DRD_FR_{year}_{ver}.xls` (in `EUROMOD-STORAGE/Data/FR/`) and read the
relevant sheet directly. Year-to-year derivations occasionally drift (new variables
added, new benefit splits introduced).
