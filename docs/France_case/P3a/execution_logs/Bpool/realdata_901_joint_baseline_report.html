Precision & standard-error asymmetry

Headline precision story. Precision is asymmetric: the shared opportunity block is tightly identified (21/29 params distinguishable from zero under the conservative clustered SE) and carries the opportunity component of any decomposition; the group-specific preference directions are wider — the flattest directions in the likelihood (identified, PD Hessian, but imprecise: 9/18 group-specific params distinguishable from zero). This is a precision feature, not an identification failure.
